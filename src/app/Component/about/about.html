About Us..

We are Creative Tech Enthusiast working since 2005

At Ghatprabha Fertilizers Pvt Ltd, we are committed to nurturing the very foundation of agriculture: the soil. Founded in 2005, we began as a local supplier with a vision to empower farmers through high-quality agricultural inputs. Over the years, we have grown into a leading manufacturer of premium NPK mixtures, soil conditioners, organic manure, and micronutrients, providing comprehensive solutions that enhance soil health and boost crop productivity.
Our mission is to enrich the land while sustaining its vitality for generations to come. We partner closely with farmers across Karnataka and Maharashtra , understanding their unique needs and delivering products that maximize yields, improve soil fertility, and support sustainable agricultural practices.
With a focus on innovation, quality, and environmental responsibility, Ghatprabha Fertilizers combines advanced manufacturing techniques with a deep understanding of traditional farming practices. Our commitment extends beyond products — we strive to educate and empower the farming community with knowledge and support, helping them achieve long-term success.
By blending science with care, we continue to cultivate growth — not just in the fields, but in the lives of farmers and the future of agriculture itself.

Our Future Goal

Sustainability & Innovation Goals

Achieve Carbon Neutrality: Commit to a low-carbon pathway in production by investing in green ammonia production (using renewable energy) and implementing carbon capture and storage (CCUS) technologies to significantly reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.
Promote Sustainable Agriculture: Become a leader in promoting the "4R" nutrient stewardship principles (right source, right rate, right time, right place) to farmers, using precision agriculture technologies (drones, soil sensors) to optimize application rates and minimize environmental impact.
Enhance Product Innovation: Allocate significant resources to R&D to develop innovative products with improved nutrient use efficiency (NUE), ensuring they are more effective and environmentally friendly than current offerings. Champion the Circular Economy: Implement processes to recover and recycle nutrients and by-products from waste streams, such as turning phosphogypsum into useful building materials or soil conditioners.
